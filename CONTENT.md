## 2. About Us

### Built on Quality. Growing Through Trust.

MRN Agro Industries was founded in 2003 by Mr. Pavan Kumar with a simple purpose: to make premium-quality rice available at affordable, market-aligned prices.

He recognised that rice is an everyday essential for countless families. His focus was not simply on producing good rice, but on delivering the same dependable quality throughout the year. That belief—that lasting success comes from quality and consistency—continues to guide our business.

From our manufacturing facility in Raichur, Karnataka, we process paddy and package finished rice under our own MRN Gold brand. Our range serves the varied needs of households, hotels, retailers, wholesalers, and distributors.

Today, we supply 250+ customers across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and Maharashtra. As we grow, we remain focused on the promise that shaped our beginnings: rice our customers can rely on, at prices that offer genuine value.

### Care at Every Stage

Our facility uses modern Satake machinery from Japan and has a paddy processing capacity of 9 tonnes per hour. Quality checks using scanners and rice laboratory testing support our focus on consistency, while hygienic packaging helps us prepare our products for supply.

Our FSSAI and AGMARK credentials reflect our commitment to food safety and quality standards.

### Our Values and Commitments

- **Quality:** Give careful attention to paddy selection, processing, testing, and packaging.
- **Consistency:** Work to maintain dependable rice quality throughout the year.
- **Honesty:** Build relationships through clear communication and fair dealing.
- **Fair pricing:** Make premium-quality rice accessible at competitive market rates.
- **Food safety:** Keep responsible handling and hygiene central to our operations.
- **Customer trust:** Support households and business customers with reliable service and supply.

Our ambition is to reach more homes and businesses across India while preserving the standards and relationships that have brought us this far.
